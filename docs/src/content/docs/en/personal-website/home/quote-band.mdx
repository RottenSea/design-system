An opening statement that fills the window: a micro-label, one large serif line, its source, and a hint at the bottom that scrolls on.

<Example system="personal-website" name="quote-band" title="A short quote on the canvas and a long one on the dark surface" viewport={1280} />

## When to use

Once, at the top of a page, in place of a hero. Not as a pull-quote inside an article.

## On a page

```astro
<TopNav … overlay />
<QuoteBand eyebrow="Writing since 2019" quotes={quotes} pullNext />
<main>…</main>
```

`quotes` is a list of `{ text, attribution }`; each visit shows one at random, and a page without script shows the first. A single quote can go in the slot with `attribution` instead. Pair it with the nav's `overlay`, so the nav takes no height and the band is a whole screen.

With `pullNext`, the element after the band rises over it faster than the page scrolls at first, easing out over the first 60% of a window, so a couple of scrolls reach it. That element gets the canvas behind it, and should be taller than a window.

## Anatomy

The uppercase micro-label, the quote in the display serif wrapped at `measure` (34 characters by default), the source in muted body text, and the hint at the bottom edge: a micro-label over a chevron.

## Variants

| Prop | Values |
| --- | --- |
| `tone` | `cream` (default), `dark`, or `bare`, which paints no background over a page that has its own |
| `overlayTone` | With `bare`: `on-dark` (default) or `ink`, for the text colours |
| `autoSize` | On by default: up to 90 characters display-lg, up to 180 display-md, then display-sm |
| `hintLabel` | `text` (default) or `none`, the chevron alone with the text as its name |
| `scrollHint` | The hint's text; `false` drops the hint |
| `minHeight` | `100svh` by default |

## Accessibility

The quote is a `<blockquote>` in a `<figure>`, with the source as its `<figcaption>`. The hint is a button that scrolls to the content, stopping below a TopNav that stays over the page so the nav does not cover its start; the arrow-only form keeps a 44px target and an accessible name. The quick rise of `pullNext` is a scroll-driven animation that a reader who asked for reduced motion does not get.
