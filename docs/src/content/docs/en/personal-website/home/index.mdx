The home page opens on a quote that fills the window, under a nav that is see-through until the page scrolls. Below it are three columns: categories and tags on the left, the posts and a pager in the middle, statistics and a calendar on the right.

<Example system="personal-website" name="home-page" title="A home page: the quote band, the filters, the posts, the stats and the calendar" viewport={1280} />

Open it at full size to scroll: the nav comes up as the page moves, and the posts rise over the quote faster than the page scrolls at first.

## How the page is put together

```astro
<TopNav brand="Field Notes" links={links} active="Blog" overlay />
<QuoteBand eyebrow="Writing since 2019" quotes={quotes} pullNext />
<main id="main">
  <ContentGrid>
    <Fragment slot="left">
      <FilterList label="Categories" items={categories} />
      <TagList items={tags} />
    </Fragment>
    <ArticleListCard … />
    <Pagination page={1} pageCount={3} />
    <Fragment slot="right">
      <SidebarCard title="Stats"><StatList items={stats} /></SidebarCard>
      <SidebarCard title="Calendar"><MiniCalendar counts={days} navigable /></SidebarCard>
    </Fragment>
  </ContentGrid>
</main>
```

The filters, the pager and the calendar only report what the reader chose, with `ds-select` and `ds-page` events; the page decides what to show.

## The parts

| Component | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| [QuoteBand](quote-band/) | The quote that fills the first screen, one of several at random |
| [ArticleListCard](article-list-card/) | One post in the list; PostMeta, the post's meta line |
| [FilterList](filter-list/) | The rail's categories; TagList, the tags |
| [Pagination](pagination/) | The numbered pager under the list |
| [StatList](stat-list/) | The site's statistics in a rail card |
| [MiniCalendar](mini-calendar/) | The month grid with the days that have posts |
