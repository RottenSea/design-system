Gives a term a one-line explanation the reader can open with a mouse, the keyboard or a tap.

<Example system="personal-website" name="abbreviation" title="Abbreviations in a sentence" />

## When to use

Use it for an acronym or a term the reader may not know, where one line is enough. Write the full form in the prose the first time as well; the tip reminds, it does not define. Leave out terms the reader uses every day, because a page full of dotted underlines is noise.

When the explanation needs links or more than one sentence, use a footnote.

## In a post

```md
Astro can also render pages on request, which is :term[SSR]{note="Server-Side Rendering"}.
```

Only the places you mark get the underline; the same term elsewhere in the post stays plain.

## Anatomy

The term, with a dotted hairline underline, and a tip on the inverse surface above it: dark on a light page, light on a dark one.

## States

Closed, and open. It opens 120ms after a mouse rests on the term, at once on keyboard focus or a tap, and closes when the mouse leaves, focus moves on, Escape is pressed or the reader taps elsewhere. The tip fades in and out; with reduced motion it appears at once.

## Accessibility

The term is a real `<abbr>` that takes keyboard focus, and the tip is linked to it with `aria-describedby`, so a screen reader reads the explanation after the term. The browser's own `title` bubble is left off, because it would open on top of the tip and never opens for the keyboard or a tap.

## API

[`Abbreviation.astro`](https://github.com/RottenSea/design-system/blob/main/systems/personal-website/src/components/content/Abbreviation.astro), which uses [`Tooltip.astro`](https://github.com/RottenSea/design-system/blob/main/systems/personal-website/src/components/feedback/Tooltip.astro). It keeps the handoff's props. The tap and the missing `title` are the two changes.
