SidebarCard is a card in a rail: an uppercase label, a soft hairline, and the widget under it. Surface is the card body that SidebarCard and every other card are made of.

<Example system="personal-website" name="sidebar-card" title="Two sidebar cards and three surfaces" />

## When to use

SidebarCard for each widget in a rail: stats, a calendar, a list of links. Surface for a container the system does not name yet, instead of a styled `<div>`.

## On a page

```astro
<SidebarCard title="Stats">…</SidebarCard>
<Surface variant="outline" hover href="/notes/">…</Surface>
```

## Anatomy

SidebarCard has 16px of padding, the label in the uppercase micro-label style, then a `--hairline-soft` rule. Rail cards stay smaller than the cards in the main column and never get a display-face heading.

Surface is the radius, the fill and the padding. Its two bodies are the system's only card bodies: `fill`, the cream card, and `outline`, the canvas with a hairline.

## Variants

| Prop | Values |
| --- | --- |
| `variant` | `fill` (default) or `outline`, for a rail that sits on a cream band |
| `tone` | `cream` (default) or `dark`, inside a dark band |
| `padding` (Surface) | `none`, `sm` (16px), `md` (24px, default) or `lg` (32px) |
| `hover` (Surface) | Lifts the card with `--shadow-hover`; nothing moves |
| `action` slot (SidebarCard) | A small link beside the label |

## Accessibility

SidebarCard is a `<section>` with its label as an `<h2>`, so the rail's cards appear in a screen reader's list of headings. A Surface with `href` is a link named by its text.

## API

[`SidebarCard.astro`](https://github.com/RottenSea/design-system/blob/main/systems/personal-website/src/components/cards/SidebarCard.astro) and [`Surface.astro`](https://github.com/RottenSea/design-system/blob/main/systems/personal-website/src/components/cards/Surface.astro). Ported from the handoff. SidebarCard leaves out `onDismiss`, because a rail card on this site is never temporary.
