The nav bar at the top of every page: the site's name, a few links, and actions on the right.

<Example system="personal-website" name="top-nav" title="TopNav with a NavDrawer" viewport={1280} />

## When to use

Once on every page, first after the skip link. Keep it to three or four links. Anything more, such as social links or secondary pages, goes in a NavDrawer under a group title.

## On a page

```astro
<TopNav brand="Field Notes" links={[{ label: "Blog", href: "/" }, { label: "About", href: "/about" }]} active="Blog">
  <ThemeToggle slot="actions" />
</TopNav>
```

`active` names the current page's link by its label. Without `menu`, the nav carries its own menu sheet with the same links. With grouped navigation, mount a NavDrawer and pass its id:

```astro
<TopNav … menu="site-menu" />
<NavDrawer id="site-menu" brand="Field Notes" groups={[{ links }, { title: "Elsewhere", links: social }]} />
```

## Anatomy

64px tall on the canvas, with no border and no shadow. The site's name in the sans at title size, then the links in the nav-link style: ink for the current page, muted for the rest. Actions sit at the right. Below 768px the links give way to a round menu button.

The menu sheet is a panel at the right of the window over a dim scrim: the site's name and a close button, the links in groups under uppercase micro-labels, and an optional note at the bottom.

## Variants

| Prop | Values |
| --- | --- |
| `linkStyle` | `sentence` (default) or `uppercase`, which draws the links as micro-labels |
| `menu` | The id of a NavDrawer to open; without it the nav has its own sheet |
| `menuGroups`, `menuNote` | Groups and a note for the nav's own sheet |
| `overlay` | Fixed and see-through over a full-height band such as [QuoteBand](../../home/quote-band/); the wash, the blur and the hairline come up together over the first `ramp` pixels of scroll (120 by default) |
| `overlayTone`, `scrolledTone` | The text over the band (`ink` or `on-dark`) and the bar's own surface once scrolled (`cream` or `dark`) |

## States

| State | What changes |
| --- | --- |
| Current page | Its link is ink and marked as the current page |
| Menu open | The scrim fades in and the panel fades in while it travels 8px; the page behind it is inert |
| Overlay, scrolled past halfway | The text takes the scrolled colours; with reduced motion the bar has only the two states |

## Accessibility

The links are a `<nav>` named "Site". The menu button says "Menu" and reports whether the sheet is open. The sheet is a modal dialog: the focus moves into it and stays there, Escape or a click on the scrim closes it, and the focus returns to the menu button. A link to another site in the sheet says that it opens a new tab.

## API

[`TopNav.astro`](https://github.com/RottenSea/design-system/blob/main/systems/personal-website/src/components/layout/TopNav.astro), [`NavDrawer.astro`](https://github.com/RottenSea/design-system/blob/main/systems/personal-website/src/components/layout/NavDrawer.astro), and [`MenuPanel.astro`](https://github.com/RottenSea/design-system/blob/main/systems/personal-website/src/components/layout/MenuPanel.astro), the sheet both use. Ported from the handoff. The links are `{ label, href }`. The handoff's sign-in and call-to-action buttons are left out; a site's own buttons go in the `actions` slot. The nav measures the scroll itself instead of taking `scrollProgress`.
