A sky of small stars behind the page. They drift slowly sideways, the nearer ones larger, brighter and faster, and each one twinkles on its own.

<Example system="personal-website" name="starfield" title="Starfield behind a heading" viewport={1280} />

## When to use

Once per page, as its background. Not inside a card or a section that scrolls past.

## On a page

```astro
<body>
  <Starfield />
  <TopNav … />
  <main>…</main>
</body>
```

It is fixed to the window and sits under everything else, so the content needs no `z-index` of its own. The system's `base.css` already paints the canvas colour under the whole page; a page that also gives `<html>` a background covers the sky with it. Surfaces with their own background, such as cards, cover the stars behind them.

With `position="absolute"` it fills its nearest positioned ancestor instead, as in the example above.

## Anatomy

One `<canvas>` the size of the window, drawn at the screen's resolution up to twice the CSS pixels. There is a star for every 5000 square pixels, placed the same way every time, so a resize keeps the pattern.

## Variants

| Prop | Values |
| --- | --- |
| `period` | Seconds a star at full depth takes to cross the width; `140` by default |
| `area` | One star per this many square pixels; `5000` by default |
| `position` | `fixed` (default) or `absolute` |

| Custom property | Default |
| --- | --- |
| `--ds-sky-star` | `--accent-teal` |
| `--ds-sky-star-strength` | `1.6` in the light theme, so the stars show on cream; `1` in the dark |

## Motion

The stars move all the time the sky is on screen, and stop when it is not. They keep moving when the reader asks for reduced motion, in the OS or with `data-reduce-motion="true"`, unlike the system's other motion: the drift is slow and even, with nothing that flashes or jumps, and the site wants its sky alive. On a theme change the stars change colour over the page's fade, or at once with reduced motion, as the page's colours do.

## Accessibility

Decoration only: the element is `aria-hidden` and takes no clicks. The stars are faint enough that text over them keeps its contrast.

## API

[`Starfield.astro`](https://github.com/RottenSea/design-system/blob/main/systems/personal-website/src/components/layout/Starfield.astro), with the drawing in [`sky.ts`](https://github.com/RottenSea/design-system/blob/main/systems/personal-website/src/components/layout/sky.ts). Not a handoff component: the handoff's The Key of Urala and Rainbow Stone pages each drew it themselves. The numbers are theirs; the colours come from tokens, and the light theme is new.
