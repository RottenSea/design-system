## Spacing

Major bands of a page sit `--space-section` apart, always the same distance; a page never tightens it. Inside cards the padding depends on the card: 32px for feature and comparison cards, 24px for content windows, 20px for grid tiles. Grids drop columns rather than shrinking cards.

## Page widths and breakpoints

Content caps at `--container-max`. A content page with both side rails uses `--container-wide` instead, which keeps the main column at the reading measure, `--container-reading`, with a rail either side. Body text never runs wider than the reading measure.

The system has two breakpoints and no others: at `--break-rails` the right rail drops, and at `--break-stack` everything stacks, with the main column first.

## Radius

Radii say what something is: 8px for buttons and inputs, 12px for content cards, 16px for a large artifact such as a hero image, a pill for badges and full for avatars and icon buttons. 4px and 6px are kept for badge accents and menu items.
