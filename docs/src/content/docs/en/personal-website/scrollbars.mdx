title: Scrollbars
description: The page's scrollbar and those inside panels keep their space, and their colour fades with the theme.
---

## Keeping the space

`base.css` sets `scrollbar-gutter: stable` on `<html>`, so the scrollbar's width is kept on the right whether the page scrolls or not. A short page and a long one centre their content in the same place, and the page does not shift sideways when the content grows past the window or a panel locks the scroll. Only the right side is kept, because keeping both would leave a strip of canvas beside every full-width dark band.

Inside the page, a scroll area keeps the space only when its content changes while it is open. SearchPanel's results do: when more results bring in a scrollbar, the rows stay where they are. The table of contents, the menu and a dialog hold fixed content and do not, or they would show an empty strip when nothing scrolls.

The docs site's example pages do not keep it either. An example never scrolls, and the strip would show the docs page behind the iframe.

## Colour

The scrollbar is thin, with the thumb in `--text-muted-soft` and a transparent track that shows the surface under it. `scrollbar-color` is inherited as a computed colour, so a scroll area inside takes the page's by default. One on a dark surface sets its own; CodeBlock uses its own soft text colour, which also reads on the navy.

## Theme changes

`--text-muted-soft` is one of the theme's variables, so the thumb fades with every other colour on the page. Browsers that ignore `scrollbar-color` draw their own scrollbar from `color-scheme`, which the theme store changes at the end of the fade, so in those browsers the scrollbar switches at the end.

## On a site

| To do | Write |
| --- | --- |
| Add a vertical scroll area whose content changes while it is open | `overflow-y: auto; scrollbar-gutter: stable;` |
| A scroll area on a dark surface | `scrollbar-color: var(--text-on-dark-soft) transparent;` |
