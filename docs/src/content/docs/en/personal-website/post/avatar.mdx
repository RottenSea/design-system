A photograph cropped to a circle, at 28, 40 or 64px. Without a photograph it shows the name's initials on a cream card.

<Example system="personal-website" name="avatar" title="Photographs and initials at the three sizes, and initials on the dark surface" />

## When to use

Where a person is named: 28px beside a meta line, 40px in a profile widget, 64px at the top of an about page. The fallback is always the initials, never a generated picture, a gradient or a person icon. Nothing goes on the avatar itself: no ring, badge or status dot.

## On a page

```astro
---
import portrait from "../assets/avatar.png";
---
<Avatar src={portrait} name="RottenTh" size="lg" />
```

## Anatomy

A circle of the given size. A photograph fills it with `object-fit: cover`. The initials are the first letters of the first two words of `name`, in the sans at weight 500 and 36% of the size.

## Variants

| Prop | Values |
| --- | --- |
| `size` | `sm` (28px), `md` (40px, default), `lg` (64px), or a number of pixels |
| `src` | An imported image, resized to the avatar's size at 1x and 2x, or a URL used as it is |
| `tone` | `cream` (default) or `dark`, the surface it sits on |

## Accessibility

A photograph takes `alt`, empty by default, since the name is usually written beside it. The initials are hidden from screen readers; the avatar is then an image named by `name`.
