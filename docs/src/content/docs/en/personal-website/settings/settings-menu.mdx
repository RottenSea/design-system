A round button that opens a small panel with the theme, as Light, Dark or System, and a Reduce motion switch. A choice applies at once and is remembered.

<Example system="personal-website" name="settings-menu" title="The settings button, then its two controls on their own: SegmentedControl and Switch" />

## When to use

Once, at the right end of the nav. It replaces ThemeToggle on a site that also wants System and Reduce motion.

## On a page

```astro
<head>
  <ThemeScript storageKey="theme-preference" motionKey="reduce-motion" />
</head>
<TopNav …>
  <SettingsMenu slot="actions" storageKey="theme-preference" motionKey="reduce-motion" />
</TopNav>
```

SegmentedControl and Switch work on their own too. Both send `ds-change` with the new value, and take a value without an event: `select(value)` and the `checked` property.

## Anatomy

A 36px IconButton with the `adjustments` icon. Its panel is 280px wide on the canvas with a hairline and `--shadow-float`, under the button with its right edge at the button's, kept inside the window. In it: an uppercase "Theme" label, the SegmentedControl, a soft hairline, then the Switch with a line of description.

SegmentedControl is a track on the cream card; the chosen option is raised to the canvas with a hairline. Switch is a 44 by 24 track, coral when on, with a canvas knob that slides over `--motion-base`.

## Variants

| Prop | Values |
| --- | --- |
| `storageKey`, `motionKey` | The localStorage keys; ThemeScript must read the same ones |
| `label` | The button's and panel's name, "Display settings" |
| `size` (SegmentedControl) | `md` (default) or `sm` |
| `tone` | `cream` or `canvas` (default), or `dark` |
| `description`, `disabled` (Switch) | A line under the label; a switch that cannot be changed |

## States

| State | What changes |
| --- | --- |
| System | The page follows the OS, including a change while it is open; nothing is stored |
| Reduce motion on | `data-reduce-motion="true"` on `<html>`: transitions and animations finish at once, page transitions stop |
| OS asks for reduced motion | The switch shows on and cannot be turned off here |

## Accessibility

The panel is a native popover named "Display settings": a click outside or Escape closes it, and the button's `aria-expanded` follows it. SegmentedControl is a radio group, not tabs, since choosing sets a value rather than showing a panel; the arrow keys move the choice and the focus. Switch is a `role="switch"` button inside its label.

## API

[`SettingsMenu.astro`](https://github.com/RottenSea/design-system/blob/main/systems/personal-website/src/components/layout/SettingsMenu.astro), [`SegmentedControl.astro`](https://github.com/RottenSea/design-system/blob/main/systems/personal-website/src/components/forms/SegmentedControl.astro) and [`Switch.astro`](https://github.com/RottenSea/design-system/blob/main/systems/personal-website/src/components/forms/Switch.astro). SegmentedControl and Switch are ported from the handoff, which has no settings panel; this one puts them in a popover the size of its DropdownMenu.
