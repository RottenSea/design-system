A round button that opens a small panel with the theme, as Light, Dark or System, and a Reduce motion switch. A choice applies at once and is remembered.

<Example system="personal-website" name="settings-menu" title="The settings button; press it for the panel" />

## When to use

Once, at the right end of the nav. It replaces ThemeToggle on a site that also wants System and Reduce motion.

## On a page

```astro
<head>
  <ThemeScript storageKey="theme-preference" motionKey="reduce-motion" />
</head>
<TopNav …>
  <SettingsMenu slot="actions" storageKey="theme-preference" motionKey="reduce-motion" />
</TopNav>
```

## Anatomy

A 36px IconButton with the `adjustments` icon. Its panel is 280px wide on the canvas with a hairline and `--shadow-float`, under the button with its right edge at the button's, kept inside the window. In it: an uppercase "Theme" label, the SegmentedControl, a soft hairline, then the Switch with a line of description.

The two controls are [SegmentedControl](../../basics/segmented-control/) and [Switch](../../basics/switch/), each on a page of its own.

## Variants

| Prop | Values |
| --- | --- |
| `storageKey`, `motionKey` | The localStorage keys; ThemeScript must read the same ones |
| `label` | The button's and panel's name, "Display settings" |
| `tone` | `cream` or `canvas` (default), or `dark` |

## States

| State | What changes |
| --- | --- |
| System | The page follows the OS, including a change while it is open; nothing is stored |
| Reduce motion on | `data-reduce-motion="true"` on `<html>`: transitions and animations finish at once, page transitions stop |
| OS asks for reduced motion | The switch shows on and cannot be turned off here |

## Accessibility

The panel is a native popover named "Display settings": a click outside or Escape closes it, and the button's `aria-expanded` follows it. SegmentedControl is a radio group and Switch a `role="switch"` button; their pages say more.

## API

[`SettingsMenu.astro`](https://github.com/RottenSea/design-system/blob/main/systems/personal-website/src/components/layout/SettingsMenu.astro). The handoff has no settings panel; this one puts its SegmentedControl and Switch in a popover the size of its DropdownMenu.
