Two buttons at the end of a post. Copy link copies the address and says so. Share poster opens a dialog with a poster of the post: the cover, the title, the description, the site and the date, and a QR code of the address, to download or hand to the system's share sheet.

It is made of three parts that work on their own:

<Example system="personal-website" name="buttons" title="Button: primary, secondary, small, text, disabled, and on the dark surface" />

## When to use

Once per post, under the copyright card.

## On a page

```astro
<PostShare title={title} description={description} url={url} site="Field Notes"
  date="2026-03-04" cover={coverUrl} filename="hairlines" />

<Button variant="secondary" iconLeft="link">Copy link</Button>
<Dialog id="note" title="A title">…<Fragment slot="actions">…</Fragment></Dialog>
<Snackbar id="toast" />   <!-- toast.show("Saved") -->
```

## Anatomy

The poster is 1080 by 1440 on the canvas of the light theme, whatever the page's theme: the cover over the top 9:16 of its width, or the cream card; the title in the display serif, up to three lines; the description in the sans; then a hairline, the site's name, the date, a coral mark, and the QR code in ink at the right. It is drawn when the dialog first opens, after its fonts have loaded.

Dialog is a centred card that swings in from the diagonal. Snackbar is one line at the bottom, on the inverse surface.

## Variants

| Prop | Values |
| --- | --- |
| `cover` | The cover's URL; without it, the cream card |
| `filename` | The PNG's name |
| `variant` (Button) | `primary`, `secondary`, `secondary-on-dark` or `text` |
| `size` (Dialog) | `sm` (420px), `md` (560px) or `lg` (720px) |
| `depth` (Dialog) | `tilt`, `lift` or `none` |

## States

| State | What changes |
| --- | --- |
| Link copied | "Link copied" for four seconds; "Couldn't copy the link" if the browser refuses |
| Sharing files possible | The dialog also has Share, for the system's share sheet |

## Accessibility

The poster's canvas is an image named after the post. The dialog is a native modal with its title as its name; Escape, the scrim and the close button close it. The snackbar is a polite live region.
